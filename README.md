# THE INTERNET DEPT.®
### Independent Digital Magazine · Est. 2026

A monochrome digital magazine exploring internet culture, digital design, technology, creativity, and the ever-changing world of being online.

Built as an independent editorial experiment, **THE INTERNET DEPT.** combines bold typography, structured layouts, and neo-brutalist design to create a reading experience that feels raw, intentional, and distinctly digital.

---

### 01 / THE PROJECT

THE INTERNET DEPT. is a responsive editorial website designed around the idea of an independent internet publication. It brings together a curated collection of sample articles in a clean, high-contrast interface inspired by print magazines, independent publications, and the early web.

**Less noise. More signal.**

### 02 / FEATURES

- **Editorial homepage** — A structured layout featuring article cards and featured stories.
- **Article reader** — Open individual articles in a dedicated reading view.
- **Category filtering** — Browse stories by editorial category.
- **Live search** — Find articles by title and relevant content.
- **Responsive design** — Adapted for desktop, tablet, and mobile screens.
- **Neo-brutalist interface** — Bold borders, monochrome colours, and expressive typography.
- **Sample editorial content** — Eight articles to demonstrate the publication's layout and reading experience.
- **Interactive navigation** — Move between the homepage and individual articles.
- **Newsletter demo** — An interactive subscription interface for the front-end experience.

### 03 / BUILT WITH

- HTML5
- CSS3
- Vanilla JavaScript
- Google Fonts

No frameworks or build tools required.

### 04 / DESIGN DIRECTION

**Visual identity:** Neo-brutalism · Editorial minimalism · Digital culture

**Colour palette:**
- Ink Black — `#111111`
- Paper White — `#FFFFFF`
- Editorial Grey — `#8F8F8F`

**Design principles:** Strong typography, clear hierarchy, generous spacing, visible structure, and functional interactions without unnecessary visual clutter.
